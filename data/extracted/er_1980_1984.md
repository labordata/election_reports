# er_1980_1984 (ER222-230, ER231-240, ER241-252)

Volumes actually span May 1980 - Nov 1982 (issues ER-222..ER-252), not 1984.

Coverage (each month once):
- Apr-Sep 1980: six-month summary in ER-226 (Sep 1980). April 1980 is covered by this summary even though ER-221 (Apr 1980 monthly) is missing, so NO GAP after ER220's Oct79-Mar80 summary.
- Oct 1980-Mar 1981: six-month summary, ER-232.
- Apr-Sep 1981: six-month summary, ER-238.
- Oct 1981-Mar 1982: six-month summary, CORRECTED ER-244-C (original ER-244 also in volume; corrected figures lower, see CSV notes).
- Apr-Sep 1982: six-month summary, ER-250.
- Oct 1982, Nov 1982: monthly (ER-251, ER-252). Dec 1982 onward is in the next volume.
- Fiscal-year rows (FY1980, FY1981, FY1982) from the one-page "Fiscal Year Summary of Elections" (preliminary, totals only, split organized/incumbent vs unorganized). FY81 and FY82 equal the sum of their six-month rows exactly. FY80 implies Oct79-Mar80 = 3618 elections / 1714 won / 234103 eligible / 89488 eligible-won; incumbent 134/59/13013/10881 - check against ER220.

Format notes: FY summary is not Tables 1/3/7; its "new" figures are the "no incumbent union involved" line (new_source=fy_summary). Monthly issues carry Tables 1 and 3 only. ER222-230 also contains a corrected Nov 1980 reissue (ER-228-C); irrelevant here since Nov 1980 is covered by the summary.

Checks: every row passes subtotal sums, won+no-rep=total (elections and eligible), and T7 = T1 - T3 (totals and one-union rows). Text layer was clean; numbers read from pdftotext and spot-checked against page images (pp.285, 470, 255).

Caveat: issues were published very late (ER-250, Sep 1982, released Nov 1986). Election volume in FY1982 (4247) is far below FY1981 (6554); monthly 1982 issues are consistent with this (~300/month), so it is as printed, not an extraction error.
