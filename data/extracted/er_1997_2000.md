## Missing pages (scan gaps) and derivations
In the issue that carries a six-month summary, the scan often lacks pages: the summary's T1/T3 and the month's own T1/T2.
- **Oct96-Mar97 (ER-424):** only six-month T4-T8 are present.
  - inc = sum of monthly T3 (Oct96 to Mar97).
  - all = T7 + inc.
- **Apr-Sep97 (ER-430):** only six-month T5-T8 are present. Derived the same way.
  - The two derived FY1997 halves sum exactly to the printed FY1997 totals, and the incumbent totals match the FY independently.
- **Oct98-Mar99 (ER-448):** only six-month T4-T8 are present. Derived the same way.
- **Apr-Sep99 (ER-454):** no six-month T1, T3 or T7 at all.
  - all = FY1999 minus the Oct98-Mar99 all.
  - inc = sum of monthly T3. It matches FY1999 inc minus Oct-Mar inc exactly.
  - new = all - inc.
  - The one-union (1u) breakdown is blank for all and new.
- **Monthly T1 pages missing:** Oct 1996, Jul 1997, Sep 1997, (Jul/Aug 1998), Dec 1998, Aug 1999, Sep 1999. They are not needed, because summaries cover those months.
