## Coverage in the CSV

- Apr-Aug 1996 are monthly rows, because the Apr-Sep 1996 summary would be in ER-418.

## Problems and derivations (scans are sideways, and pages are missing or bound out of order)
- **ER-394 (Apr-Sep 94):** the six-month T1-T6 pages (xviii-xxiii) are missing from the scan, and so are the Sept 94 monthly T1/T2. Only T7/T8 are present. `inc_*` is the sum of the six monthly Table 3s. It equals FY1994 incumbent minus the Oct93-Mar94 six-month T3 exactly. `all_* = new + inc`.
- **ER-400 (Oct 94-Mar 95):** six-month T1 and T7 are present, T3 is missing. `inc = T1 - T7`. This matches the sum of the monthly T3s exactly, including the one-union row.
- **ER-406 (Apr-Sep 95):** six-month T1-T6 are missing, and so are the Sept 95 monthly T1/T2. Same treatment as ER-394, cross-checked against the FY1995 summary.
- **ER-412 (Oct 95-Mar 96):** six-month T1-T3 are missing. Pages 110-112 are the March 96 monthly tables, bound out of order. `all_*` and `inc_*` are sums of the monthly T1/T3 for Oct95-Mar96. Monthly T1 minus T3 reproduces the printed six-month T7 exactly (all 8 numbers).
- In every case where a printed six-month table and the sum of monthly tables can both be checked, they agree exactly. So the derived rows should match what the missing pages printed.

## Format change
From ER-390 (May 1994) the tables no longer have a separate Teamsters column. Affiliation columns are just AFL-CIO and UNAFF., because the Teamsters (AFL-CIO since 1987) are now counted as AFL-CIO. ER-381..389 still use AFL-CIO / Teamsters / Other Unaff. The totals are unaffected.

- Apr 1996 one-union eligible-won: the image reads like 7,983. I recorded 7,988, which is 7,327+661 and makes the 8,526 total work.
- Jul 1996 one-union elections: the image reads like 213. I recorded 218, which is 198+20, makes the 225 total work, and gives 49.1% = 107/218.
