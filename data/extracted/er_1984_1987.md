## Notes / format
- Issues were published out of order. For example, the Oct83-Mar84 summary appears in the Aug 1984 issue (ER-273), not in the Mar 1984 issue.
- The FY summaries are one-page "Fiscal Year Summary of Elections" tables, marked PRELIMINARY, BEFORE ADJUSTMENTS. They have Organized (incumbent) and Unorganized (no incumbent) lines only, with no union-count breakdown. Each one equals the sum of its two six-month summaries exactly. The unorganized line is put in new_* with new_source=fy_summary_unorganized.
- Some monthly-table page headers in ER-265 (Dec83) read "JUNE 1984", and those in ER-271 (Jun84) read "DECEMBER 1983". These are header misprints or binding swaps. Both months are inside summaries and were not used.
- All required checks pass for every row: subtotals sum to total, won + no-rep = total, and T7 = T1 - T3 for both counts and eligible employees.
- OCR fixes:
  - Apr-Sep85 T1 one-union n: OCR read 1,601; the correct figure is **1,801**.
  - Apr-Sep85 T3 total n is smudged; it is **153**.
  - May86 pages are scanned inverted and mirrored. OCR read T1 total eligible 15,011, eligible won 5,331 and no-rep 13,630; the correct figures are **16,011 / 5,381 / 10,630**. OCR read T3 two-union eligible won as 593; it is **590**.
  - All of these were set using row sums, affiliation columns and cross-table identities.
- Two very large single elections: an 18,200-employee three-union election won by the AFL-CIO in Oct83-Mar84 (new organizing), and a 4,458-employee Teamsters-incumbent election in Apr-Sep84.
