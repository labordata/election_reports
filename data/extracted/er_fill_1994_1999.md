# er_fill_1994_1999: printed six-month tables recovered from the extra Google Books volumes

Sources:
- GB_-gszFLAvRi0C (Stanford copy, ER-397..414)
- GB_ouh0MMgcucgC (ER-386..397)
- GB_URRoXQGFT4wC (U. Michigan copy, ER-446..457)

All numbers were read from page images rendered at 130-170 dpi and rotated.

## Filled (printed values found)
| Period | Issue | Pages (GB_-gszFLAvRi0C) | What was found | Matches rebuilt row? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oct94-Mar95 | ER-400 | 152-159 | T3 is printed, along with T1-T8 | Yes. 50/26/3,432/2,559; one-union 37/16/1,574/837 |
| Apr-Sep95 | ER-406 | 441-448 | T1, T3 and T7 printed | Yes, all 24 values |
| Oct95-Mar96 | ER-412 | 704-711 | T1, T3 and T7 printed | Yes, all 24 values |

Checks pass for all three periods:
- subtotals sum to the totals;
- won + no-rep = total elections;
- eligible-won + eligible-no-rep = total eligible;
- T1 - T3 = T7, for the totals and for the one-union rows.

The rebuilt rows in er_1994_1997.csv for these periods are therefore confirmed exactly. Their basis can be relabeled as printed.

## Not fillable
- **Apr-Sep94 (ER-394):** GB_ouh0MMgcucgC p384 has the issue, but the same pages are missing as in our scan:
  - six-month T1-T4 are absent;
  - the Sep 1994 monthly T1/T2 are absent;
  - only six-month T7/T8 (pp405-406) and T5/T6 (pp411-412) are present.

  This second, independent copy has the same gap, so the pages were probably never printed in this issue (an original printing omission).
- **Oct98-Mar99 (ER-448):** GB_URRoXQGFT4wC p84. Six-month T1-T4 are absent here too. pp104-107 are the March 1999 monthly T1-T4 (paged xxv-xxviii), and pp108-111 are six-month T5-T8.
- **Apr-Sep99 (ER-454):** GB_URRoXQGFT4wC p360. Only six-month T8 and T5/T6 are present, plus FY1999 (p378). Six-month T1, T3 and T7 are absent, and so is the Sep 1999 monthly T1, the same as in our scan.
- **Oct96-Mar97 (ER-424) and Apr-Sep97 (ER-430):** none of the extra volumes covers Jul 1996 - Dec 1998.
