# er_1987_1989 memo

## What the files contain (all issues present, no gaps)
- ER295-309.pdf: ER-295 (Jun 1986) through ER-309 (Aug 1987), one issue per month. ER-298 (Sep 1986) has the Apr-Sep 1986 six-month summary and the FY1986 summary; ER-304 (Mar 1987) has the Oct 1986-Mar 1987 summary.
- ER310-323.pdf: ER-310 (Sep 1987; Apr-Sep 1987 summary and FY1987) through ER-323 (Oct 1988). ER-316 (Mar 1988) has the Oct87-Mar88 summary; ER-322 (Sep 1988) has the Apr-Sep88 summary and FY1988.
- ER324 Nov 1988, ER325 Dec 1988, ER326 Jan 1989, ER327 Feb 1989, ER328 Mar 1989 (Oct88-Mar89 summary), ER329 Apr 1989, ER330 May 1989.
- ER1989.pdf: ER-331 Jun 1989, ER-332 Jul, ER-333 Aug, ER-334 Sep 1989 (Apr-Sep89 summary and FY1989).
- ER335: Oct 1989 (monthly only).
No missing issues. No corrected reissues; the "Revised" strings in the text are form-revision footers.

## Coverage in the CSV
- Six-month summaries: Apr-Sep86, Oct86-Mar87, Apr-Sep87, Oct87-Mar88, Apr-Sep88, Oct88-Mar89, Apr-Sep89. Together they cover every month from Jun 1986 through Sep 1989. Apr-Sep86 also includes Apr-May 1986, whose issues are not in this block.
- Fiscal-year summaries for FY1986-FY1989, to use as checks. They have a different format: one "Summary of Elections" page with incumbent and no-incumbent lines and no one-union breakdown. For these rows new_source is fy_summary_no_incumbent.
- Monthly: Oct 1989 (ER-335). Monthly issues have no Table 7, so the new_* fields are blank.

## Checks and fixes
- Every six-month row passes all checks: subtotals add to the total, won plus no-representative equals the total, and T7 = T1 - T3 for both the total and the one-union rows.
- FY1987 and FY1988 equal the sum of their two six-month rows exactly. FY1989 agrees on everything except the printed no-incumbent elections figure of 3640, which should be 3460 (3669-209, and 1671+1789). It is recorded as printed.
- ER-322 T1: the one-union eligible-won figure is printed truncated as "38,86". It is reconstructed as 38860 from its components and from 95012-56152.
- ER-328 T1: the text layer reads the one-union eligible figure as 134,295. The image and the arithmetic give 104,295.
- ER-335 T3: the one-union won figure is printed as 6, but its component rows (5+3) and the grand total of 9 imply 8. It is recorded as printed and flagged in checks.
- ER-335 T1: the total elections figure renders as "30b" and is 308 (304+4).
- All numbers were read from page images (110-300 dpi). The pages in this block are upright, not sideways.
