# er_1976_1980: NLRB Election Report summaries, FY1977 through H1 FY1980

Files: ER184, ER190, ER196, ER202, ER208, ER214, ER220. Each is a single issue (the March or September report) that carries a six-month summary (Summary Tables 1-8) followed by that month's own tables.

## Coverage
Six-month summaries: there are 7 rows, covering Oct 1976 through Mar 1980 continuously with no gaps.
- Oct76-Mar77: ER-184, pdf pp.19, 21, 24
- Apr-Sep77: ER-190, pp.20, 22, 26
- Oct77-Mar78: ER-196, pp.20, 22, 26
- Apr-Sep78: ER-202, pp.22, 24, 28
- Oct78-Mar79: ER-214 ERRATA, pp.6, 8, 12. These are corrected tables that replace the ones printed in ER-208.
- Apr-Sep79: ER-214, pp.34, 36, 40
- Oct79-Mar80: ER-220, pp.22, 24, 28

Fiscal-year summary rows are check rows only, so do not count them twice:
- FY1977: ER-190 p.19
- FY1978: ER-202 p.21
- FY1979: ER-214 p.30

These FY pages are a single small table with three rows: Organized (incumbent), Unorganized (no incumbent) and Total. They are marked "Preliminary, before adjustments" and have no affiliation breakdown, so the 1u fields are blank. new_source is "fy_summary".

No monthly rows were emitted. The March and September monthly tables in these files fall inside the summary periods.

## Corrections and errata
- **ER-208's Oct78-Mar79 summary was superseded.** ER-214 prints all 8 tables again under the heading ERRATA. The note on p.4 says they "replace those issued in the March election report (ER-208)."
  - The originals in ER-208 were far smaller. Table 1 showed 2134 elections and 174,588 eligible, against 3148 and 250,275 in the corrected version.
  - The corrected figures were used. FY1979 equals the corrected Oct-Mar plus Apr-Sep exactly.
- The ER-220 ERRATUM (p.21) concerns one case in the Feb 1980 listing. It does not affect the summary tables.

## Check results
All subtotal, won + no-representative, and Table 7 = Table 1 - Table 3 checks pass, with one exception: Apr-Sep 1977 (ER-190).
- Printed Table 7 is lower than Table 1 - Table 3 by 2 elections, 2 won, 1,921 eligible and 1,921 eligible in units won.
- The gap is entirely in the two-union rows:
  - AFL-CIO v. Teamsters: 1 election, 41 eligible
  - AFL-CIO v. Other Unaff.: 1 election, 1,880 eligible
- The one-union and three-or-more rows reconcile exactly.
- The FY1977 summary uses the Table 7 figures (total 8,633 rather than 8,635), so it shows the same gap against the sum of the six-month Table 1s.
- The numbers are recorded as printed.

## OCR and image notes
- These pages are upright. Values were read from 130-dpi page images and cross-checked against the text layer.
- The ER-190 Table 1 and ER-202 Table 1 pages are faint or blurred. Their values were confirmed with the text layer and with the arithmetic identities, which all hold.
- One value was derived rather than read: ER-202 one-union won (1,768) is the sum 1,040 + 533 + 195. It is consistent with 3,747 - 1,979.
- Format change: from ER-190 onward the summary tables are computer printouts headed "NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS BOARD", while ER-184 is typeset. The two-union row labels also change from AFL-CIO*/Teamsters* ordering to separate incumbent-first rows. The column structure is the same.
