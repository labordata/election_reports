ER145 (Dec 1973), ER169 (Dec 1975), ER175 (Jun 1976), ER178 (Sep 1976). Each is a single issue.

Rows emitted (12):
- Nine summary rows: six-month Jul-Dec 1968, Jan-Jun 1971, Jul-Dec 1971, Jul-Dec 1972, Jan-Jun 1973,
  Jul-Dec 1973, Jul-Dec 1975, Jan-Jun 1976, and the three-month Jul-Sep 1976 transition quarter (ER178).
  Each comes from Summary Tables 1, 3 and 7 (new_source=table7).

  the "Unorganized" row (new_source=fy_summary). These are for checking only.

Jan-Jun 1974 through Jan-Jun 1975. Because of this, FY1971 has only its Jan-Jun half here.
FY1973 and FY1976 have both halves.
